01 · Business Problem

Pricing Decisions Made on Gut Feel

Pricing analysts juggle spreadsheets, stale competitor screenshots, and instinct — with no fast way to answer "what happens to profit if I drop this SKU 10%?" or "how confident should I even be in this recommendation?"

Blind to Elasticity

Analysts change prices without a live view of how demand actually responds — margin and volume trade-offs are guessed, not modeled.

Blind to Competitors

Market position (premium vs. competitive) is discovered too late — by the time a price gap is noticed manually, revenue has already leaked.

Blind to Data Quality

Every recommendation looks equally confident on a dashboard — even when it's backed by thin, seasonal, or promo-contaminated data.

The Goal

Give a Senior Pricing Analyst one workspace: simulate, converse, monitor, and commit — with the AI's confidence made visible, not assumed.

05 · Pricing Strategies

Three Objective Functions, One Model

Every module — Price Optimizer, Insight Engine, Mix Management — can be reweighted toward one of three business objectives, so the same elasticity data supports very different commercial goals.

📣 Market Share

Optimize for volume

Prioritizes demand capture and competitive positioning over per-unit margin — pushes price toward the lower end of the elasticity curve to win share.

📈 Profit Maximization

Optimize for margin × volume

Solves for the price that maximizes total contribution dollars, trading some volume for a meaningfully higher margin — the default in the walkthrough above.

🛡️ Protective

Optimize for stability

Biases toward smaller, lower-risk price moves — built for SKUs where the Data Confidence layer flags thin or promo-contaminated signal.
